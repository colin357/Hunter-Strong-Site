VA Home Loans

The short answer

A VA loan lets eligible veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members and some surviving spouses buy a home with $0 down and no monthly mortgage insurance. Hunter Strong, a loan officer with FirstTrust Home Loans (NMLS #1911747), helps military families in Arkansas, Mississippi, Tennessee and 10 other states use their VA benefit, from first-time VA buyers to repeat users.

VA loan benefits

  • No down payment with full entitlement
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • Competitive pricing because the loan is backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs
  • Limits on certain closing costs, and sellers can contribute toward your costs
  • Reusable: you can use the benefit again, and in some cases have more than one VA loan

The VA funding fee

Most VA borrowers pay a one-time funding fee, which can be rolled into the loan. The amount depends on your down payment and whether it's your first VA loan. Veterans receiving VA disability compensation are generally exempt, as are some surviving spouses and Purple Heart recipients on active duty.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility depends on your length and type of service: regular military, Reserves or National Guard, or a qualifying surviving spouse. Hunter can request your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) for you, usually within minutes.

VA refinancing

Already have a VA loan? A VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) can lower your rate with limited paperwork. A VA cash-out refinance can tap equity or move a non-VA loan into VA. See refinancing.

Frequently asked questions

Do VA loans really require no down payment?

Yes. Eligible borrowers with full entitlement can buy with $0 down, subject to credit and income qualification. You'll still have closing costs, which seller concessions can often help cover.

Is there a minimum credit score for a VA loan?

The VA doesn't set a minimum credit score, but lenders do. Hunter will review your credit and tell you exactly where you stand.

Can I use my VA loan benefit more than once?

Yes. Your entitlement is restored when you sell and pay off a VA loan, and in some cases you can have more than one VA loan at the same time.

Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?

Generally, veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, eligible surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, and active-duty Purple Heart recipients.
