New Construction Home Loans

The short answer

Buying a newly built home works differently than buying an existing one: longer timelines, builder contracts, rate locks that may need to be extended and sometimes construction financing. Hunter Strong, a loan officer with FirstTrust Home Loans (NMLS #1911747), helps buyers in Arkansas, Mississippi, Tennessee and 10 other states plan for new construction, from a spec home in a subdivision to a custom build.

Buying from a builder

If the builder is constructing the home and you buy it at completion, you'll typically use a standard purchase loan (conventional, FHA or VA). The main differences are timing and rate protection, because the home may not be finished for months. Hunter will talk through rate-lock options so a rate change during the build doesn't wreck your budget.

Building on your own land

Custom builds may need construction financing that converts to a permanent mortgage when the home is finished. Requirements depend on the builder, the plans and the program, so bring your builder, budget and timeline to Hunter early.

New construction tips

  • Get pre-approved before you sign a builder contract.
  • You're allowed to use your own lender, even if the builder recommends one. Compare the full offer, including any builder incentives.
  • Budget for upgrades, landscaping, window coverings and appliances that may not be included.
  • Get an independent inspection, even on a brand-new house.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use the builder's preferred lender?

No. You can choose your own lender. Builders sometimes offer incentives for using their lender, so compare the full picture (rate, fees and incentives) before deciding. Hunter is happy to give you a side-by-side.

Can I lock my rate while my home is being built?

Extended rate locks are available in many cases and may cost more the longer the lock. Hunter will help you time the lock around your expected completion date.

Can I use an FHA or VA loan on new construction?

Often, yes, as long as the home and builder meet program requirements. Talk to Hunter before you sign so the paperwork is set up correctly.
