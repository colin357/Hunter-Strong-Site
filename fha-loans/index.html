FHA Loans

The short answer

An FHA loan is a government-insured mortgage that allows 3.5% down with a credit score of 580 or higher and more flexible debt-to-income limits than most conventional loans. Hunter Strong (NMLS #1911747, FirstTrust Home Loans) helps buyers in Arkansas, Mississippi, Tennessee and 10 other states decide whether FHA or conventional gives them the better overall deal.

Who is an FHA loan good for?

  • First-time buyers with smaller down payments
  • Buyers with credit scores in the 580–680 range
  • Buyers carrying student loans or car payments that push their debt-to-income ratio up
  • Buyers getting gift money from family for the down payment
  • Buyers who want a 2–4 unit property and plan to live in one unit

FHA loan basics

  • Down payment: 3.5% with a 580+ score; 10% with a score from 500 to 579.
  • Mortgage insurance: an upfront premium (usually rolled into the loan) plus a monthly premium.
  • Property: must be your primary residence and meet FHA appraisal standards.
  • Loan limits: set by county each year.
  • Seller help: sellers can contribute toward your closing costs.

FHA vs. conventional: how Hunter decides

With a strong credit score, conventional PMI is often cheaper than FHA mortgage insurance and can be removed later. With a lower score or tighter budget, FHA usually wins. Hunter prices both and shows you the monthly payment and cash to close side by side.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum credit score for an FHA loan?

FHA guidelines allow 3.5% down with a 580 score and 10% down with a score from 500 to 579. Individual lenders may require higher scores, so ask Hunter where you stand.

Can I get rid of FHA mortgage insurance?

With less than 10% down, FHA mortgage insurance generally lasts for the life of the loan. Many borrowers refinance into a conventional loan once they have about 20% equity to remove it.

Can I use an FHA loan more than once?

Yes. FHA isn't only for first-time buyers. You can generally have one FHA loan at a time on your primary residence.

Can my down payment be a gift?

Yes. FHA allows your entire down payment to come from an acceptable gift source, such as a family member, with a signed gift letter and documentation.
