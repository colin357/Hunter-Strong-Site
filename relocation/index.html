Relocation Home Loans

The short answer

Relocating means juggling a job start date, a home to sell and a home to buy, often from a different state. Hunter Strong, a loan officer with FirstTrust Home Loans (NMLS #1911747), is licensed in 13 states (Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Missouri, Mississippi, Ohio, Oklahoma, Tennessee, Texas, Wisconsin) and helps relocating buyers get pre-approved remotely, coordinate timelines and close on schedule.

By Hunter Strong, Loan Officer, FirstTrust Home Loans · Updated November 7, 2026

Get Pre-ApprovedCall (870) 917-9396

How Hunter helps relocating buyers

  • Remote-friendly process: secure online application, digital documents and phone or video calls.
  • Job offer income: in many cases a signed offer letter can be used to qualify before your first paycheck.
  • Buying before you sell: options for qualifying while you still own your current home.
  • Military PCS moves: VA loans with $0 down for eligible service members. See VA loans.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get pre-approved before I move?

Yes. The entire pre-approval can be done remotely through Hunter's online application, so you can shop with a pre-approval letter from out of state.

Can I qualify with a new job I haven't started yet?

Often, yes. Many loan programs allow a signed offer letter with a start date shortly after closing. Hunter will check the requirements for your situation.
